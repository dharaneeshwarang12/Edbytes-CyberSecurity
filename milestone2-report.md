# Milestone 2 – Everyday Security and Phishing Checks

## 1. Analysis of Fictional Messages

**Message 1: Urgent Payment Request**
- Suspicious feature: Urgent payment to a mismatched bank account.
- Explanation: Attackers may create urgency to make people transfer money without verification.
- Uncertainty: It could be genuine, so verify through a known official contact.

**Message 2: College Login Password Request**
- Suspicious feature: Asking for a password through an unverified message.
- Explanation: This may be a phishing attempt to steal login credentials.
- Uncertainty: Verify with the college IT department through an official channel.

**Message 3: Unexpected Invoice Attachment**
- Suspicious feature: Unexpected invoice attachment.
- Explanation: It may contain malicious files or links.
- Uncertainty: Confirm with the sender before opening it.

## 2. Five Personal Security Actions

1. Use unique and strong passwords for every account.
2. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
3. Regularly update operating systems and applications.
4. Check website links before clicking.
5. Maintain secure account recovery options.

## 3. Security Awareness

Never share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information. Always verify suspicious messages through trusted channels.
## 4. Normal Case and Failure Case Testing

**Normal Case:**
- Verified a message from a known contact through an official channel.
- Result: Message was treated as safe after verification.

**Failure Case:**
- Received an unexpected invoice attachment.
- Action: Avoided opening it and verified the sender.

**What I Learned:**
I learned how to identify phishing messages and protect personal information.
